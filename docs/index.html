NCR Transition Matrix Lab

Change how people move between Delhi, Gurugram, Noida and Faridabad and watch the long-run distribution, the eigenvalues and the 2011–2061 projection update live. Columns are “from”, rows are “to”; each column sums to 1, so the diagonal (people who stay) is filled in for you.
